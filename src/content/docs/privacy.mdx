## What this site is

These docs are built with [Astro Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build) and served as static files from [pratyay360.github.io](https://pratyay360.github.io/). There is no backend, no database, and no user accounts on this site.

Source code lives at [`Pratyay360/pratyay360.github.io`](https://github.com/Pratyay360/pratyay360.github.io) on GitHub.

## Data we collect directly

**Nothing.**

We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information to read these docs. We do not run analytics, A/B testing, advertising pixels, or cross-site trackers on these pages.

If you use the **Edit this page** link, you leave this site and interact with GitHub under GitHub's own privacy policy.

## Hosting and server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may record basic technical logs (such as IP address, requested URL, date/time, and user agent) for security, abuse prevention, and reliability. We do not combine these logs with anything else and do not use them to identify you.

## Third-party embeds

These pages include or link to a small number of third parties. They only see your data if your browser loads them or you click through:

- **Contact form (Youform):** The `Contact us` button in the footer links to [https://app.youform.com/forms/moki0tve](https://app.youform.com/forms/moki0tve). Anything you type there is handled by Youform, not by us. Only contact us there if you are comfortable with that.
- **Website Carbon badge:** The footer loads a badge script from `unpkg.com` (`website-carbon-badges`). That CDN will see a standard request (IP, user agent) when the footer loads, same as any CDN-hosted script.
- **External links:** GitHub, `pratyay.qzz.io`, jsDelivr (used by some guide examples such as `upiqrcode`), and similar outbound links are governed by those sites' own policies once you click them.

We do not sell or share personal data with anyone — there is nothing to sell or share.

## Cookies and local storage

We do not set tracking or advertising cookies, and there is no cookie-consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Your browser may store purely functional, first-party preferences locally (for example, Starlight's light/dark theme choice). This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Search (Pagefind) runs entirely in your browser against a pre-built index.

## Children's privacy

This site is general technical documentation and is not directed at children. Since we do not collect personal data in the first place, we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 16.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the `Last updated` date above. Material changes will be visible in the page history on GitHub.
