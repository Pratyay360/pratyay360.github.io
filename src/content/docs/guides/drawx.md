## What is DrawX?

[**DrawX**](https://github.com/Pratyay360/drawx) is an offline-first native desktop application bringing the full power of Excalidraw directly to Linux, macOS, and Windows.

Unlike cloud-based diagramming tools that upload your canvases to remote servers, DrawX executes 100% locally on your machine. Your drawings, sensitive system architectures, product mockups, and personal brainstorming canvases never leave your computer unless you explicitly export them.

---

## Why Choose DrawX?

- 🔒 **Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee**: No external network requests, analytics, or telemetry. Works completely air-gapped without an internet connection.
- ⚡ **Instant Launch & Native Performance**: Lightweight desktop runtime with near-instant boot times and smooth 60fps canvas panning and zooming.
- 🔄 **Seamless Auto-Updates**: Built with CrabNebula Cloud integration to ensure smooth, secure, and automatic background updates across all operating systems.
- 📦 **Standard File Formats**: Save, open, and export files in native `.excalidraw` format, vector SVG, or high-resolution PNG.
